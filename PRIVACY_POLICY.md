# Privacy Policy for MS CureChain Patient App

**Last Updated: April 23, 2026**

MS Tech Hive Private Limited (“MS Tech Hive”, “we”, “us”, “our”) owns and operates the MS CureChain Hospital Management System and its associated mobile applications. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how it is protected.

## 1. Information We Collect
We collect information to provide better services to all our users.
- **Personal Information**: Name, Age, Gender, and Contact Details.
- **Health Data**: Appointment history, Medical records, Lab reports, Prescriptions, and Vitals.
- **Usage Data**: Device information, IP address, and Push tokens.

## 2. App Permissions
The app requests certain permissions to function correctly:
- **Camera**: Required for scanning Doctor/Hospital QR codes to facilitate quick appointment booking and for document uploads.
- **Location**: Used to find nearby hospitals and provide emergency SOS services.
- **Notifications**: Used to send appointment reminders, status updates, and health alerts.

## 3. How We Use Information
- To enable and manage appointment bookings.
- To provide secure access to your health records.
- To facilitate Emergency SOS and hospital location services.
- **We DO NOT sell your personal health data to third-party marketers.**

## 4. Data Storage & Security
- Data is hosted primarily within India on secure servers.
- We implement industry-standard encryption for data at rest and in transit.
- Access is restricted via strict role-based controls.
